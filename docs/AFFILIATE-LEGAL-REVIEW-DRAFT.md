# Affiliate program: questions for legal and finance review

This is a review brief, not an approved legal opinion or a customer refund policy. The owner has asked to launch Geraina and DapurOS first; DagangOS Web is deferred. The affiliate terms currently shown to users are version `2026-11-17-v1`.

## Owner's business decisions

- One affiliate commission on the first qualifying payment per merchant and product. Renewals, plan changes and migrations do not earn another commission.
- The commission is 10% of the first purchased package's standard monthly price, including for an annual purchase. The qualifying first purchase receives a 10% customer discount. Trial registrations alone earn no commission.
- Attribution remains valid for 30 days from referral link to signup and 90 days from signup to qualifying first payment.
- Payouts are reviewed and paid monthly on the 25th, Western Indonesia Time, once the available balance reaches Rp149,000 and the relevant payment is at least 14 days old.
- The owner intends monthly subscription fees to be nonrefundable. For an ordinary partial refund of an annual plan, the original first-month affiliate commission is retained. Confirmed full refunds, transaction reversals, chargebacks and other exceptional cases must be reviewed separately.

## Questions to resolve before adopting a customer cancellation policy

1. Review whether and how the proposed monthly nonrefundability and annual cancellation calculations may be stated and applied, including mandatory consumer remedies and any other required exceptions. The supplied PRD explicitly places the proposed cancellation wording behind an Indonesian legal review gate.
2. Review when an earned affiliate commission may be held, adjusted, reversed, paid or recovered after an exceptional refund, chargeback, fraud, or a payout already made; ensure affiliate and customer documents agree.
3. Review required disclosure and acceptance of discounts, the trial, paid-plan start, auto-renewal, cancellation, affiliate advertising relationships and changes to program terms.
4. Determine what identity, bank ownership, tax/withholding and record-retention procedures DagangOS must apply for affiliates, and who may access the evidence. Current email verification and the displayed last four bank digits do not independently establish identity or account ownership.
5. Review complaint, dispute, appeal and contact procedures, including what information can be shared with an affiliate without disclosing a merchant's private payment details.

The current system holds ambiguous or pending refunds for review, retains the affiliate commission for a verified annual partial refund, and reverses it on a confirmed full refund or reversal. It does not implement a blanket denial of customer refunds. A reviewer should assess the actual wording and implementation together before approving a final policy.
